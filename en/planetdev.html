Planet Development

Your home planet has six buildable facilities — Trade Port, Shipyard, Defense Satellite, Laboratory, Bar and Military Command — each upgradable from level 1 to 15. Facility levels connect to the economy by nudging the planet's five indicators (R/P/D/T/E) a little in each daily batch.

Design document vs actual tables — this page follows the actual tables The original design document (_000_09_DESIGN_FACILITY_UPGRADE_SYSTEM_v2_0.md, 2026-06-19) specifies levels 1–10 and build conditions based on combat wins (Trade Port 10 wins, Shipyard 5, Laboratory 15, Bar 8), but the tables actually in use (v3.1/v3.2) extend levels to 15 and remove every combat-win condition (only the installation wait remains). Following the Table-First principle, this page uses the real CSV values — if you see numbers that differ from older design documents, this page is the newer one.

The six facilities at a glance

Trade

Trade Port

Lower trade fees · higher stock cap · more high-end weapons in stock. Pushes R and P.

Ships

Shipyard

Unlocks buildable hull classes · raises the mineral upgrade cap · shortens build time. Pushes T.

Defense

Defense Satellite

More satellites, DPS and invasion interception. D indicator (drops instantly when destroyed, rises when invasions are repelled).

Research

Laboratory

Faster R&D unlocks · environment regeneration · intel detection, counter-terror and drone interception bonuses. Pushes T and E.

Social

Bar

More bounty board slots · reputation bonus · unlocks mercenary grades. Pushes P.

Command

Military Command

Logistics discount on its own upkeep · raises D and T targets at a small cost to R, P and E. Territorial defense doctrine is planned for endgame.

The daily upkeep table also mentions another facility, population_dome, but it has neither a level policy table nor a design document, so its actual effect cannot be confirmed — we list it as a facility that exists only in data.

Defense Satellite

Lv1: 1 satellite · DPS 200 · 5% interception → Lv10: 5 satellites · DPS 150,000 · 95% interception. When a satellite is destroyed the D indicator drops immediately, and repelling an invasion raises it — unlike the Trade Port, Shipyard, Laboratory and Bar, it moves its indicator in both directions.

Military Command

A "territorial doctrine" facility that works separately from the Defense Satellite. It does not change satellite counts or interception. Two effects are live in the game today.

  • Logistics discount Live — cuts the Military Command's own daily upkeep by up to 12% depending on level (combined cap of 22% with the global upkeep discount; does not apply to Defense Satellite upkeep)
  • Indicator offsets Live — in the daily batch, shifts the targets the indicators settle toward by D +2 · T +1.5, but R −1 · P −1 · E −1.2 at Lv15 (never below the planet's original baseline or 8, whichever is higher). This is separate from the other facilities' stat nudges
  • Territorial defense doctrine Planned for endgame — designed as a defender bonus in automatic territorial battles (+0.4% at Lv1 to +6% at Lv15), with the formula already in place, but it will be connected when endgame content begins and does not apply in the game today

What happens every day — stat nudges

Facility levels do nothing in real time. Only in the daily 12:00 KST batch do they push indicators up by level × coefficient (capped at 100, paused while the planet is unoccupied).

Upgrade times and instant completion

Upgrade time grows exponentially with level — Lv1→2 takes 45 minutes, Lv4→5 one day, Lv9→10 thirty days and Lv14→15 sixty days. Each facility has its own speed factor (Trade Port and Bar are slightly faster, the Laboratory 10% slower).

Instead of waiting, you can complete instantly with credits — it costs an extra ~40% of the upgrade cost (50% of the installation cost for the first build). Pushing everything through with instant completion from start to finish costs 3–5× the base cost overall.

Build conditions

On any planet you occupy, you can start building any facility right away with no combat-win condition — only the installation wait (15–30 minutes). Upgrading requires pilot levels and certain indicator thresholds.

FacilityLv5 conditionLv8 condition
Trade PortPilot Lv15Pilot Lv30
ShipyardPilot Lv20Pilot Lv40
BarPilot Lv15 + Population (P) 30Pilot Lv30 + Population (P) 55
LaboratoryPilot Lv20 + Technology (T) 30Pilot Lv40 + Technology (T) 55
Military CommandPilot Lv20 + Defense (D) 30Pilot Lv40 + Defense (D) 55

Because of these thresholds, the facilities have no direct prerequisites on each other but share the same pool of five indicators and so push each other indirectly — the T and P raised by the Trade Port and Laboratory meet the next-step conditions for the Bar and Laboratory.

Development aurora (2026-10-21)

The planet shown at the hub gains a soft band of light (an aurora) around it as you develop it. The score is the sum of the six facility levels (0–90); an untouched planet with only its default facilities shows nothing. The aurora switches off during combat.

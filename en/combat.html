Combat · Conflict

Combat is not one system but four distinct axes, while the galaxy's balance of power shifts separately through an automatic rotation every 20 minutes. This page covers those four axes and the contested-zone system that changes the map.

Combat level (TCL) — what difficulty actually means

Every planet has a target combat level (TCL) between 1 and 60. But TCL actually changes only enemy weapon loadouts and encounter/wave invader tiers. Enemy ship HP, armor and shields are fixed in the ship CSV regardless of TCL, and all 30 tiers of reserve wave invaders share HP 80 · shield 40 · armor 8 — the sense that the game gets harder later on comes mostly from weapon swaps and a growing number of simultaneous enemies.

Four level scales run separately TCL (for weapons and encounter selection) · recommended pilot level (for pacing) · the level shown on the map (cosmetic, often out of line with TCL) · actual captain and ship combat levels (not rescaled by TCL) — these four can show different numbers for the same planet. Example: Helios Core shows level 9 on the map, but its actual TCL is 37.

Drone and fighter combat

Drones do not hover in front of the ship in advance. They wait, then launch one at a time from the bay → fly straight at the enemy → circle once near the target → ram it, with a fiery explosion on impact (improved 2026-10-03). Fighters launch from the carrier → attack several times in a figure-eight path → return to the carrier (at its current position) when time runs out. The existing missile Bézier curves cannot express this, so they are implemented separately with fixed slot pools of 8 drones and 8 fighters — see Weapons.

Contested zones — why "conflict" splits three ways

The galaxy map moves through a campaign rotation that visits one planet every 20 minutes (the sub-core only checks every 60 seconds whether "it's time"). However, the word "contested zone" is used with three different definitions in the code — a structural flaw identified as the root cause of the map changing while the economy and the feel of play fail to keep up.

DefinitionUsed forScope
A. Seed conflictDaily "aftermath" stat changes · skipping internal affairs (WDI/rebellion)Only 3: Draco, Omega and Shadow Market
B. Campaign conflict20-minute rotation · NPC auto-battles · map ring markersA's three + Helios · Titan + dynamic promotions
C. Dynamic conflictFilling the pool (8–12) · war spoils eligibilityPlanets promoted as front lines or strategic neutrals

As a result, "aftermath" (planet stat changes) applies only to three planets, leaving a blind spot where visibly front-line planets like Helios and Titan get neither aftermath nor internal-affairs changes.

Cooldown formula: demotion cooldown laps × active pool size × 1200 s. With a pool of 8, about 5.3 hours.

Capital defense

Each of the four faction lanes has one "capital" planet — New Eden in the west (Stellium), Arcfire Core Prime in the east (Crimson), while the south and north (Mercurium/Aurelium) are only designated as Synth planets for future expansion. While at least one neighboring system is friendly, the capital stays fully immune (auto-invasion, neutral declaration and pool promotion are all blocked). Only when friendly neighbors drop to zero and a hostile neighbor appears does it become open to siege — and even then the defender gets a favorable multiplier. This system applies only to auto-battles and does not affect the win/loss rules of wave battles the player fights directly.

Stellium annexation — turning neutral planets blue

Players can annex a neutral core planet into Stellium Union (blue) territory (re-enabled 2026-10-03). When every condition is met, a "Join the Stellium Union" button opens in the planet info panel.

  • You must be landed on that planet
  • The planet has a Defense Satellite of Lv 1 or higher
  • At least one neighboring system is friendly blue territory
  • Pay 8,000 cr from the blue shared vault (not your wallet)
  • Endgame planets (Eternity Throne · Genesis Origin) are excluded

Annexation applies only once, when you confirm it in the info panel (never automatically on tick or boot), and the vault payment is refunded on failure. Policy values are tuned in tables/balance/stellium_annex_policy.csv.

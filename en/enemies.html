Enemy Ships

The named enemy captains you meet across the galaxy and the warships they fly. Each captain has a home base and an operating area, and turns up in transit encounters, planet waves and contested-zone battles armed for that level.

By faction

Enemy fleets are armed groups that act apart from the four nations. Even within one faction, regions and level bands differ.

All enemy captains

Search by captain, ship, faction, location or weapon. Sorted by combat level.

About the weapons column An enemy ship's actual loadout is picked at each sortie from its combat level and role (assault, balanced, sniper, carrier, firepower). "Typical weapons" shows the strongest weapon each family can field at that level — in a real fight a ship carries about two of these families.
Quest-only enemies are left out Enemy captains and ships that only appear in quest stories are not listed, so the plots stay unspoiled.

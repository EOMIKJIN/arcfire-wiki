The Four Factions

The galaxy is split among four mega-factions (nations), one for each lane direction. Only two of them currently have implemented content — the Stellium in the west and the Crimson in the east. The south and north are planned expansions with only their names and lanes decided.

Western lane · the player's nation

Stellium Union Active

The side defended by the player and Stella Aris. Its capital is New Eden (New Eden Prime). Blue on the map.

Southern lane

Mercurium Union Planned

Only its name, lane and capital (Mercurium Prime) are set. Actual gameplay axes such as southern fleet operations are not live yet.

Eastern lane · hostile power

Crimson Legion Active

The hostile side that discovered ArcCore and is invading the Stellium with its power. Its capital is Arcfire Core Prime. Red on the map.

Northern lane

Aurelium Guild Planned

Only its name, lane and capital (Aurelium Prime) are set. Northern fleet operations are not live yet.

Not a war that "isn't happening yet" From the intro cutscene onward, the Crimson invasion of the Stellium is set as ongoing — the moment you start the game, you are already in the middle of the war.

Regional affiliations (per planet)

Separate from the four factions, every planet and NPC has a finer regional affiliation — the Federation, the Federation military, the Miners' Guild, the Trade Coalition, scientists, pirates, independents, raiders, the Border Watch, void wanderers and more. For example, the starting planet Arcadia is listed as "Federation", Minerva Deep as "Miners' Guild" and the Crimson Zone as "Pirates". If the four factions are the big borders, these are the actual governance and cultural colors at the planet level.

Independent nation — the player's status

There is a separate "independent nation" status that is neither blue nor red. Planets the player holds count as part of this independent nation, which has its own independent-nation vault. Resetting your account resets only this vault to zero and returns your holdings to neutral.

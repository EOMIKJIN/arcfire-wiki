Bar · Captains · Player

It's a single-player game, but the galaxy is home to more than 300 named NPC captains and to the "bar", a social space found on planet after planet. You, too, start out as a pilot with a backstory.

The bar — a social hub

The design document states that the bar is "main content, not a side menu". By design, capital-class planets get more — and flashier — attendants. The current table lists one or two attendants at the bars of 96 planets, core and Synth combined.

  1. Enter the bar → the host NPC starts an in-game conversation: "Buy me a drink?"
  2. Accept → the attendant roster appears
  3. Pick one and buy a drink (costs credits)
  4. A performance session begins — one drink = 15 real-time minutes. Dialogue pages and a short looping animation unlock
  5. [Another drink] extends the time and unlocks the next stage of content (1 drink = greeting, 2 = personal story, 3 = dance clip + longer conversation…)

"Popularity" is measured by total credits spent, not by the number of drinks. There are three drinks — House Ale, Nebula Wine and Phospho Fizz — and only five songs, shared by every attendant; each attendant's personality comes from eight dialogue archetypes plus a unique name and portrait. Attendant dialogue is not generated by ArcCore's LLM — that is explicitly out of scope.

Content rating The design document sets "no sexual content and no depiction of minors" as a firm principle.
Design target 288 vs 100 actually registered The design document describes a galaxy-wide target of 288, but a direct count of bar_attendants.csv shows 100 attendants registered in active systems today (103 rows minus 3 in disabled systems). This wiki follows the real table, not the document's target.

NPC captains — 324

As of 2026-10-15 there are 324 public captains — by combat side, 58 blue · 159 red · 107 non-combat. Captains you meet only in quests (144) and test captains are left out to avoid spoilers and confusion. There are 313 portraits (some captains share one), shown in the first column of the table below. The 100 bar attendants share 22 portraits.

These 324 captains are independent characters with a rank (admiral, colonel, fleet leader, warlord and so on), affiliation, AI temperament (cautious to berserk) and role (fleet leader, patrol, escort, garrison, hunter, merchant guard). Observed types:

  • Fleet commanders and admiral-class leaders
  • Border patrol and escort captains (e.g. guarding mining convoys)
  • Garrison and port commanders
  • Research and science captains — e.g. Vector-7, the captain of an experimental AI hull that responds only to anomalous energy signals
  • Hostile warlords and pirate raid captains (many numbered Vega red/blue)
  • Wreck-field looters (Iron Cross remnants)
  • Three AI clan flagship leaders — Adeline Rook (safe zone), Lin Kendall (neutral zone) and Mora Shayer (contested zone), each representing a zone's fleet

Twelve of them form the "civilian orbit filler line" — named captains purely for traffic volume.

Permanent death — designed, but nobody dies yet Regular patrol, wave-spawn and contested-zone captains are explicitly immortal (exempt) — the risk is too high. Permanent death targets only "designated main-story captains", and so far none has been designated. The system (ledger and gates) is already implemented, but no one is tagged as "able to die" yet.

Tarot destiny system Planning

The concept is "a first choice that decides the future" — before choosing a character, you set your "destiny" by picking one of 22 Major Arcana–style cards directly by number (1–22). It is a fixed principle that the interpretations of the 22 cards are written by the owner, not by AI. The first scope covers only the presentation (cards scatter, and choosing a number flips one over to reveal its reading); connecting it to actual stats and story branches is deferred to a later stage.

Tutorial Foundation design

A forced, linear onboarding on Arcadia, the first planet you land on. While it runs, it is designed so that the tutorial is the only thing in the foreground — every other menu, inbound alert, wave and rebellion alarm is locked. There is intentionally no skip button. The only voice in this section is Stella Aris.

  1. A. Survival loop — welcome → scan → mine → check cargo → open the trade port → actually sell one item
  2. B. Ships — just browse the shipyard (no forced purchase; you already own a starter ship)
  3. C. People — pick one person from the [Talk] list and browse the bar board (accepting is locked)
  4. D. Wrap-up — closing lines → departure unlocked → the first main mission actually begins here

Weapons

Five families — lasers, missiles, rockets, drones and fighters — for a total of 103 entries (83 sold in shops + 20 test-only). Tiers rise in the order Basic → New → Veteran → Elite → Legendary → Epic → Transcendent → Divine → Cosmic, spanning levels 1 to 60.

The five families

27 types

Lasers

17 in shops + 10 test. Instant-hit beam weapons.

35 types

Missiles

The largest family, mostly guided missiles.

19 types

Rockets

Multi-launch and area projectiles.

12 types

Drones

7 in shops + 5 test. Three-step behavior: approach → orbit → charge.

10 types

Fighters

5 in shops + 5 test. Launch → repeated figure-eight attacks → return.

Drones and fighters are "units", not projectiles A normal weapon ends at fire and hit. A drone instead closes on its target, circles at a set radius and finally charges once and self-destructs; a fighter launches from its carrier, loops around the target in a figure-eight (lemniscate) path attacking several times over a set duration, then returns to the carrier (or to wherever the carrier has moved). The Bézier curves used for missiles cannot express this behavior, so they are implemented separately with dedicated slot pools (8 drones and 8 fighters — fixed pools with no new allocation per tick).

Tier ladder

The higher the required level, the higher the tier, and prices climb exponentially — a level 1 Basic laser deals 4 damage for 1,430 cr, while a level 60 Cosmic laser deals 60 damage for 7,043,000 cr.

Flavor text vs actual behavior

Most of the 103 weapons hint at flashy special effects in their names and descriptions — ignoring shields, piercing armor, EMP, stopping time, erasing coordinates — but very few actually implement such effects in numbers. We note the gap clearly here.

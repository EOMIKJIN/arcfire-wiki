Weapons

Five families — lasers, missiles, rockets, drones and fighters — for a total of 99 weapons (98 sold in shops + 1 not for sale). Tiers rise in the order Basic → New → Veteran → Elite → Legendary → Epic → Transcendent → Divine → Cosmic, spanning levels 1 to 60. Internal test weapons (20 VMock clones and 2 wave-test weapons) are not listed in this wiki.

The five families

16 types

Lasers

15 in shops + 1 not for sale. Instant-hit beam weapons.

40 types

Missiles

The largest family, mostly guided missiles, including the Interceptor Missile (Defense tier).

19 types

Rockets

Multiple-launch and area-effect projectiles.

13 types

Drones

Three-stage behavior: approach → orbit → ram.

11 types

Fighters

Launch → repeated figure-eight attack runs → return.

Drones and fighters are "units", not projectiles A normal weapon ends at fire and hit. A drone instead closes on its target, circles at a set radius and finally charges once and self-destructs; a fighter launches from its carrier, loops around the target in a figure-eight (lemniscate) path attacking several times over a set duration, then returns to the carrier (or to wherever the carrier has moved). The Bézier curves used for missiles cannot express this behavior, so they are implemented separately with dedicated slot pools (8 drones and 8 fighters — fixed pools with no new allocation per tick).

Flavor text vs actual behavior

Most of the 99 weapons promise flashy special effects in their names and descriptions — ignoring shields, piercing armor, EMP, stopping time, erasing coordinates — but very few actually implement them in the numbers. This section spells out the gap.

WeaponFlavor textActual behavior
Nova MissileWide-area explosionLive A 60px-radius AoE at the target point plus a slow — the only weapon with a real area check

Ships

You progress by buying hulls at the shipyard across 13 tiers, from the starting scout up to the top-tier "Apex Legend". This wiki lists 327 ships, but underneath there are only six hull archetypes — the ships differ only in name, faction and role.

Player hull ladder

Thirteen shipyard tiers. A mid-step "Corvette Mk.I" was added between the Striker and the destroyers on 2026-10-13. From destroyers onward, a brawler (main line) and a scout (alternate line) are sold side by side at the same tier — scouts have lower HP and shields but use more agile hulls. The table below lists the two lines on separate rows, and ships of the same tier are paired with an orange tag "Same tier L(required level) · partner ship" — same-tier ships share the required level and price and differ only in stats and hull. The "Apex" battlecruisers are the top of the battlecruiser line and can now be bought outright. The Super Capital and Apex Legend are long-term goal ships that do not appear in the shipyard's ladder guide. The "Survival Pod" is an emergency hull that is not sold in shops.

Six hull archetypes

All 327 player and NPC ships listed here share one of the six hulls below.

patrol

The most common light-to-medium patrol frame (161 ships)

line

The capital-ship frame of the player's top line (74 ships)

raider

An agile raiding frame (60 ships)

siege

A siege frame for cruisers up to dreadnoughts (13 ships)

carrier_light

Light carriers such as the "Survival Pod" (10 ships)

research

Research frames such as the "Blue Fleet" (9 ships)

NPC fleets and factions

NPC ships come in role-based name variants for each home system — Invader, Interdictor, Pursuer, Raider, Hunter and so on. Eleven home systems are registered: Arcadia, Draco Nebula, Solar Port, Minerva, Vega Outpost, New Eden, Iron Cross, Omega Station, Helios and Sirius, plus the contested-zone clan "Nightfall (Crimson Moon)".

Other ships — 304 ships

NPC ships across the galaxy (companions, faction fleets, enemy ships and more). The 22 player ships in the "Player hull ladder" above and the Survival Pod are left out, as are quest-only ships, test/mock-up ships and the wave reserve ships. Home system, hull and stats for each ship.

Wave-defense enemies

In wave defense, the enemy fleet is built from the ships of the hostile captains assigned to that planet (true of all 21 core planets since 2026-10-07). The 30 tiers of shared invaders (npc_wave_invader_t1–t30) are only a reserve for planets without their own enemies, so they are left out of this list. For how difficulty scales, see Combat · Conflict.

Loadout slots — 3 weapons · 3 equipment · 4 skills (2026-10-21)

SlotsNumbersRule
Weapons1–3Lasers, missiles, rockets, drones or fighters. You cannot equip the same weapon twice (and only one slot per family).

Skills7–10S1 · S2 active · S3 · S4 passive — see Skills.

For now every ship uses the same ten slots regardless of hull tier. All other slots are closed and will open later along with new special weapons and items and an inventory screen.

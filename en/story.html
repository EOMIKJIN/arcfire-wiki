Story · Missions

The main story is designed as ten chapters, and all 30 main quests of Chapter 1, "Concealed Truth", are implemented (as of 2026-10-05). This wiki is honest about what actually exists and what is still empty (Chapters 2–10).

Intro — starting the game

A three-page cutscene presents, in turn, the Crimson invasion, the Stellium resistance and the existence of ArcCore. It then leads into character (class) selection → nickname entry → the warp sequence → landing on Arcadia → Stella Aris's tutorial guidance.

Chapter 1 — Concealed Truth

Light Hold's last lullaby Twenty years ago, the frontier colony of Light Hold was deliberately abandoned as bait by the Stellium Union's leadership and fell within 47 hours. The last transmission it sent was a parent's lullaby, singing a child to sleep. Chapter 1, "Concealed Truth", runs this buried past in parallel with an ongoing investigation into an "unmotivated murder case".

Chapter 1 continues through 30 main quests, story_001–story_030. It starts with the first quest, Unmotivated Murder Case (client: Ellen de Cor), passes through the names Light Hold left behind, three allies joining and the Shadow Nexus siege, and closes with End of Concealed Truth. Each quest opens only after a required-level gate (Lv 1 → 7 → 9 → 15 → 25 → 28 → 32 → 36 → 40 → 44), and the gaps between gates are filled with side jobs and training.

Chapters 2–10

Each chapter has a skeleton that can hold up to 30 main quest slots, four branch slots and a chapter-ending cutscene, but the actual scenarios have not been written yet — even their titles are placeholders such as "Chapter 2 (scenario pending)". This wiki does not invent content that does not exist.

Early planet route

The early travel order fixed by the onboarding and tutorial track.

Arcadia (home) → Vega Outpost → Solar Port (Chapter 1's first lead) → Minerva Deep (mining and delivery) → Draco Nebula (candidate for Chapter 1 stage 2, not yet started) → New Eden (entry point to the open world) → Omega Station (end of the tutorial track)

Future narrative structure — three archetypes

Separate from the three onboarding classes (Ren Kaze · Mia Velo · Kyle Drake — see the player page), three narrative archetypes are mentioned as the structure for continuing the unstarted parts of the main story: Firepower Commander · Trade Master · Pioneer Strategist. They are a high-level design concept not yet implemented as concrete quests.

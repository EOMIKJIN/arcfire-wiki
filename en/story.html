World · Narrative

Story · Missions

The main story is planned as ten chapters, and all 30 main quests of Chapter 1, "Concealed Truth", are implemented (as of 2026-10-15). So that you can discover the story yourself, this page covers the main story only up to chapter titles and a short premise — no quest titles, plot details or character reveals.

Intro — starting the game

A three-page cutscene introduces, in turn, the Crimson invasion, the Stellium resistance and the existence of ArcCore. It then leads into character (class) selection → nickname entry → the warp sequence → landing on Arcadia → Stella Aris's tutorial guidance.

Chapter 1 — Concealed Truth

Premise A murder with no apparent motive takes place on a border planet, and you are drawn into the investigation. The trail leads back to the records of Light Hold, a frontier colony that fell twenty years ago — what happened that day is for you to find out.

Chapter 1 runs through 30 main quests that unlock step by step from Lv 1 to Lv 44. Side jobs and training fill the level gaps between quests.

Chapters 2–10

Each chapter has a framework that can hold up to 30 main quest slots, four branch slots and a chapter-ending cutscene, but the actual scenarios have not been written yet — even the titles are placeholders such as "Chapter 2 (scenario pending)". This wiki does not invent content that does not exist.

Side job list — 106 missions

Every mission except the main story — 5 tutorial missions · 76 side jobs given by planet NPCs · 25 bar board job templates. To avoid spoilers, only the title, region and recommended level are listed.

Early planet route

The early travel order set by the onboarding and tutorial track.

Arcadia (home) → Vega Outpost → Solar Port → Minerva Deep (mining and deliveries) → Draco Nebula → New Eden (gateway to the open world) → Omega Station (end of the tutorial track)

Future narrative structure — three archetypes

Separate from the three onboarding classes (Ren Kaze · Mia Velo · Kyle Drake — see the player page), three narrative archetypes are mentioned as the framework for the unwritten parts of the main story: Firepower Commander · Trade Master · Pioneer Strategist. They are a high-level design concept, not yet built into actual quests.

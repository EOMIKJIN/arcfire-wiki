ARCFIRE

A single-player space strategy game: you take a single ship through trade, combat and faction wars in a galaxy that an AI runs on your behalf. There are no other players on the server, but the galaxy keeps turning — ArcCore, a resident AI, runs a batch once a day. Even while you are offline, factions rise and fall, trade lanes fill up or go quiet, and some planets change hands.

About this wiki This is an unofficial guide to the game, compiled from a full survey of the project's design documents and content tables (CSV). The code and numbers keep changing, so treat everything here as a snapshot taken on the survey date (updated 2026-10-15, first published 2026-09-18). Quest-only characters, test data and disabled star systems are left out, and the main story is covered only up to chapter titles to avoid spoilers. The game itself currently supports Korean and English; tables use the in-game English names.

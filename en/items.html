The 10 minerals

In order of rarity: Ferrite → Silicate → Carbonaceous → Nickel → Titanium → Platinum → Energy Crystal → Orichalcum → Neutronium → Voidstone. Base prices rise from 10 to 420. They are consumed by shipyard upgrades.

Shipyard upgrades

Upgrades are saved per ship and cannot be transferred to another ship (reworked 2026-10-10). There are eight upgrade tracks — hull HP · shield · turn speed · laser damage · laser fire rate · missile damage · missile fire rate · cargo hold — and each level costs both credits and minerals. Per-level effects and costs depend on the ship's tier: fully upgrading a ship brings it to about 160% of its original stats (about 87.5% of the next tier's base model), for a total cost of about 80% of the next tier's purchase price.

The maximum upgrade level is the lowest of three caps: your pilot level, the Shipyard facility level and the ship's tier. Pilot level caps: Lv 15 or below → +5 · 29 or below → +8 · 43 or below → +10 · 59 or below → +12 · above → +15.

Planet ownership deeds (99 types) The largest group in any item category (most of the 138 luxury goods). There is one for each of the 21 core planets and 78 Synth planets. Fixed at 12,000 cr and not resellable — buying one makes that planet the property of the player's clan.

The 12 diplomatic gifts

Each of the three nations — Crimson Legion, Mercurium and Aurelium — has four: a credential, a trade accord, a monument and an insignia. They are diplomatic documents and keepsakes whose effects switch on one by one as your intimacy with that nation reaches levels 1, 2, 3 and 4. They cannot be traded or sold.

The twelve-seat relics

There are twelve "Pantheon relics", such as relic_seat_chronos, matching the twelve seats of ArcCore. They have no combat or economic effect; they are obtained only by salvaging wrecks and unlock codex entries — collectible lore items.

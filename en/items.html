The 10 minerals

In order of rarity: Ferrite → Silicate → Carbonaceous → Nickel → Titanium → Platinum → Energy Crystal → Orichalcum → Neutronium → Voidstone. Base prices rise from 10 to 420. They are consumed by shipyard upgrades.

Shipyard upgrades

Pick one of five slots — hull armor, shield cells, engine core, weapon mods or energy — and spend a primary mineral (60%) and a secondary mineral (40%) to raise its level. The amount needed grows as 5 × next level − 2 (level 1 = 3, level 8 = 38), and the maximum upgrade level is capped by your pilot combat level (≤14 → +5 · ≤30 → +8 · ≤50 → +12 · above → +15). Each level adds fixed bonuses such as max HP +50, max shield +30 and turn rate +3%, and laser and missile weapons also gain damage, reload and range bonuses.

Planet ownership deeds (102 types) The largest share of any item category (most of the 129 luxury goods). There is one for each of the 21 core planets plus about 45 Synth planets. Fixed at 12,000 cr and not resellable — buying one makes that planet the property of the player's clan.

The twelve-seat relics

There are twelve "Pantheon relics", such as relic_seat_chronos, matching the twelve seats of ArcCore. They have no combat or economic effect; they are obtained only by salvaging wrecks and unlock codex entries — collectible lore items.

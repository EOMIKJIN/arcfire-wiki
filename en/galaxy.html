Galaxy · Planets

There are 21 playable core systems, linked in a mesh graph where each system connects to two to four neighbors. You start in the safe zone, and the danger rises as you head from west to east.

Zone tiers

Safe

Zones 1–4. Starting areas such as Arcadia and Solar Port

Neutral

Zones 5–10. First-gate areas such as the Draco Nebula

Contested zone

Zones 11–19. High-risk areas such as Blood Field and Nightfall

Endgame

Zones 19–21. Arcfire Core, Eternity and Genesis

The three gates

  • Draco Nebula (Zone 5) — the first gate. Your first boss encounter, the first shielded enemies, and a nebula thick with electromagnetic interference
  • Blood Field (Zone 11) — the second combat gate. The first heavily armored enemies, on a battlefield covered in fleet wreckage from a past war
  • Abyss (Zone 18) — the Crimson's last line of defense. Near a black hole, and it must be broken through to reach the Arcfire Core

Synth colonies — 76 planets

Besides the 21 core planets, there are additional planets that ArcCore's automatic colonization protocol opens up one after another. Three of the base 79 (Uncharted-7, -12 and -38) are switched off on the galaxy map to rework travel routes, so 76 currently appear. Only some of them are named in advance; the rest are generated as play progresses. Many of the planet ownership deeds on the items page correspond to these Synth planets.

Mineral regions

The core planets and Synth planets fall into two mineral regions — the Core Trade Belt, centered on safety and trade (Arcadia, Vega, Solar, Minerva and others), and the Frontier Rim, neutral and high-risk (most of the rest). See Economy for how mining works.

The bigger picture — some 700 systems The full galaxy graph already holds roughly 757–760 systems, but only these 21 "core" systems are permanently open right now. The rest remain unexplored frontier, and you can pass all four gateways leading out to it once you learn the Navigation skill Star Pathfinder (since 2026-10-20; per-gateway quest conditions can also open them) — the structure for long-term expansion is already in place.
